DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
─────────────────────────────
Forte is the fitness social app that actually keeps you accountable.

Log workouts, track your macros, compete in weekly leagues, and share your progress with people who push you harder.

TRAIN SMARTER
• Log every workout in seconds — sets, reps, weight, and rest time
• Forte detects your personal records automatically and celebrates them
• Full exercise library with hundreds of movements
• Build and save custom workout templates

EAT BETTER
• Log meals and track calories against your personalised daily targets
• Macro targets calculated from your body stats and goals during setup
• Calorie, protein, carb and fat tracking in one clean view

COMPETE HARDER
• Join weekly leagues with friends and compete for the top spot
• New season every Monday — points earned by logging workouts
• Create public or private leagues for your gym crew

STAY SOCIAL
• Share workout milestones and progress photos with your community
• Follow friends, react to posts, and leave comments
• A social feed that motivates instead of distracts

EARN YOUR BADGES
• 15+ achievement badges — from logging your first workout to setting 10 PRs
• Maintain streaks to unlock harder badges
• Weekly recap every Monday showing your stats for the week

BUILT DIFFERENT
• Dark, distraction-free design made for the gym
• Works as a full-screen app — add to your Home Screen for the best experience
• Your data stays yours — delete your account and all data at any time

Forte is designed for people who are serious about their training. No fluff, no filler.
