Frequently asked questions

How do I log a workout?
Tap the + button in the centre of the bottom navigation bar. Choose "Log workout," then select exercises, add sets and reps, and save. Your personal records are tracked automatically.
How do I track my nutrition?
Go to the Nutrition tab at the bottom. You can log meals, search foods, and track your calories and macros against your personalised daily targets. Targets are calculated during onboarding and can be updated in your profile settings.
What are leagues?
Leagues are weekly competitions with friends or public groups. Points are earned by logging workouts. A new season starts every Monday. Join or create a league from the + button.
How do I find friends?
Tap the search icon on the Feed screen to find users by name or username. You can also follow people directly from posts in the Community feed.
How do I earn badges?
Badges are awarded automatically when you hit milestones: logging your first workout, maintaining streaks, setting personal records, and more. Check your Profile to see all earned and locked badges.
How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" below the password field. Enter your email and we'll send you a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.
How do I delete my account?
Go to your Profile, then Settings, then scroll to "Delete Account." This permanently removes all your data including workouts, nutrition logs, and posts. This action cannot be undone.
How do I report a user or post?
Tap the three-dot menu on any post or profile and select "Report." Choose the reason and submit. Our team reviews all reports within 48 hours. You can also block a user to prevent them from seeing your content.
How do I turn off notifications?
Go to your device's Settings app, find Forte, and toggle notifications off. You can also manage specific notification types inside the app via Profile, then Settings, then Notifications.
